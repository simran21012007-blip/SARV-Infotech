A TRIBUTE TO A VISIONARY

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Scientist | Teacher | President | Dreamer

A remarkable Indian scientist and former President who dedicated his life to knowledge, innovation and inspiring the youth of India.

ABOUT

A Scientist Who Inspired a Generation

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists, educators and public figures. His journey from a modest childhood in Rameswaram to becoming the President of India reflected his determination, discipline and belief in the power of education.

Beyond his scientific achievements, he became especially known for connecting with students and encouraging young people to think boldly, work sincerely and contribute to the development of the nation.

LIFE JOURNEY

From Rameswaram to Rashtrapati Bhavan

1931

A Humble Beginning

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Kalam grew up in a simple family and developed a strong interest in learning.

1960

Beginning His Scientific Career

He began his professional journey as an aerospace scientist and became involved in India's developing space and defence programmes.

1969

India's Space Programme

At ISRO, he contributed to the development of India's satellite launch vehicle programme, helping strengthen the country's space efforts.

1998

Pokhran-II

He played a significant role in India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests and became a prominent figure in the country's strategic and scientific community.

2002

The People's President

He became the 11th President of India. His approachable personality and connection with young people earned him widespread affection.

2015

A Lasting Legacy

He passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong, remaining dedicated to education until the end.
